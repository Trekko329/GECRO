🚚 Open route
Prototype testing only. Public OpenStreetMap geocoding and OSRM demonstration routing services are used. Match confirmation, delivery reopening, Undo, saved route progress, and an interactive route map are included for testing. They have usage restrictions and are not approved as a production courier service. Test with public or dummy addresses only, not customer details. Results are estimates without live traffic.
